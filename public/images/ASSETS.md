# Design reference assets

Both images were generated with the built-in image_gen tool for the SurgaltHub V2 design revision. They depict illustrative content, not a real student or center. WebP copies are used by the app; PNG sources are retained.

## Study hero

Saved asset: `public/images/study-hero-v2.webp`

Prompt:

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: panoramic website hero background for a Mongolian education course directory. Create a wide 3:1 realistic editorial photograph: young adult East Asian woman with long dark hair, wearing a cream knitted sweater, seated at a warm wooden study desk, writing with a pen in an open notebook, laptop at right, bookshelves and a few green plants behind. Composition: woman occupies right half, face at 70% of width, upper body and notebook visible, left half is softly out-of-focus library space with no people, uncluttered for a dark gradient and headline overlay. Warm natural daylight, professional natural photography, navy shadows and warm beige highlights. No text, no interface, no logos, no watermark.

## Language illustration

Saved asset: `public/images/language-globe-v2.webp`

Prompt:

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: transparent illustration for a blue-and-white language course directory. Primary request: a polished friendly 3D globe with bright blue oceans, green continents, a small stack of three cream books with red and yellow covers below, and four floating rounded speech bubbles around its upper-left side, pastel blue, coral, white, white, labeled respectively A, 文, 가, あ. Glossy soft clay materials, subtle shadows, professional education website style. Composition: isolated compact arrangement, globe on right, bubbles on left, books at bottom; no background, true transparency. No extra text, no logos, no people, no watermark.
